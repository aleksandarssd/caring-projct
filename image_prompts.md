These prompts are highly optimized for professional AI image generators like **Midjourney v6**, **DALL-E 3**, or **Stable Diffusion 3**. They are designed to create imagery that perfectly matches the "human, warm, trustworthy, and calm" brand identity, strictly avoiding the sterile "stock photo" or "hospital" aesthetic.

### 💡 Tips for Best Results:
1. **No Uniforms:** AI tends to put caregivers in blue scrubs. Always specify "casual everyday clothes" or "no medical uniforms" to keep it feeling like everyday assistance, not a hospital.
2. **Lighting:** "Soft natural window light" and "warm golden tones" will perfectly match your `#FAF8F4` cream and `#3D6B52` green color palette.
3. **Age & Dignity:** Use words like "elegant," "gentle," and "smiling softly" for the elderly subjects to ensure they are portrayed with dignity and respect, not as frail or sad.
